    SLID User Manual

    Welcome to SLID

    SLID is your unified digital identity platform, bringing together all your social media accounts in one clean, user-friendly interface. With SLID, you can manage multiple social media platforms, interact with your connections, and navigate your digital presence seamlessly using our AI-powered tools.

    This manual will guide you through all the features of SLID and help you make the most of your digital identity management.

    Getting Started

    What is SLID?

    SLID (Social Linked Identity Dashboard) is a platform that integrates your social media and online accounts into one user-friendly interface. It serves as your digital ID, centralizing and unifying your digital presence across platforms.

    Key Features: - Unified social media experience - AI-powered data navigation - Digital identity management - Cross-platform integration - Secure authentication

    System Requirements

    SLID is a web-based application that works on: - Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) - Desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

    Account Setup

    Creating a New Account

    1. Visit SLID website
    2. Click the "Sign Up" button
    3. Enter your desired username, email address, and password
    4. Click "Create Account"
    5. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your email
    6. Accept the terms and conditions

    Setting Up Your Profile

    After signing up, you'll be prompted to complete your profile:

    1. Add your full name
    2. Upload a profile picture
    3. Write a short bio
    4. Set your profile visibility preferences

    Your profile completeness will be displayed as a percentage. A complete profile enhances your SLID experience and helps others find and connect with you.

    Connecting Social Media Accounts

    Supported Platforms

    SLID currently supports these social media platforms: - Facebook - Instagram - X (formerly Twitter)

    How to Connect an Account

    1. From your dashboard, click on "Connected Accounts"
    2. Select the platform you want to connect
    3. Click "Connect"
    4. You'll be redirected to the platform's authorization page
    5. Log in to the platform if needed
    6. Grant SLID permission to access your account
    7. You'll be redirected back to SLID once connected

    Permissions and Privacy

    When connecting accounts, SLID will request only the permissions it needs to function: - Basic profile information - Content access - Post creation capabilities (optional)

    SLID does not: - Post without your permission - Access private messages - Store your platform passwords

    You can review and modify these permissions at any time.

    Navigating Your Profile

    Dashboard Overview

    Your SLID dashboard is organized into these main sections:

    Profile Summary - Your profile completeness score - Quick statistics from connected accounts - Recent activity

    Connected Accounts - List of all connected social platforms - Connection status for each platform - Quick actions for each platform

    Content Feed - Aggregated content from your accounts - Content creation tools - Filter options

    Connections - People you're connected with on SLID - Connection recommendations - Connection management

    Personalization Options

    Customize your SLID experience:

    1. Click on "Settings" in the top-right menu
    2. Under "Appearance," choose your theme preference
    3. Under "Dashboard," arrange widgets as you prefer
    4. Set your default view for when you log in

    Managing Content

    Viewing Your Content

    SLID aggregates content from all your connected platforms:

    1. Go to the "Content" tab
    2. Use filters to sort by:
    3. Platform
    4. Date
    5. Content type (images, videos, text)
    6. Engagement metrics

    Creating Content

    Create and share content across multiple platforms:

    1. Click "Create" in the content section
    2. Choose content type (text, image, video)
    3. Upload media or write your text
    4. Select which platforms to post to
    5. Click "Post" to share across selected platforms

    Analytics and Insights

    Track how your content performs:

    1. Click on any post to view detailed analytics
    2. See engagement metrics across platforms
    3. Compare performance between different platforms
    4. View audience demographics (where available)

    Using AI Assistant

    What the AI Can Do

    SLID's AI assistant helps you navigate your digital presence:

    • Answer questions about your social media activity
    • Find specific posts or interactions
    • Identify patterns in your content
    • Make recommendations for improving engagement
    • Help you discover connections across platforms

    Making Queries

    To use the AI assistant:

    1. Click the AI icon in the navigation bar
    2. Type your question in natural language
    3. Press Enter or click "Ask"

    Example queries: - "Show me my most popular Instagram posts from last month" - "When did I last interact with Jane on Facebook?" - "Which of my posts mentioned climate change?" - "Find connections I have on both LinkedIn and Twitter"

    Improving AI Results

    For better results:

    • Be specific about time periods
    • Mention specific platforms when relevant
    • Use names of connections you're asking about
    • Provide context if the AI misunderstands

    Managing Connections

    Finding People

    Find people to connect with:

    1. Go to the "Connections" tab
    2. Use the search bar to find people by name or username
    3. Browse recommended connections based on:
    4. Mutual connections
    5. Connected platforms
    6. Content interests

    Making Connections

    To connect with someone:

    1. Visit their profile
    2. Click the "Connect" button
    3. Optionally add a personal message
    4. Wait for them to accept your connection request

    Managing Your Network

    Organize and manage your connections:

    1. Visit the "Connections" tab
    2. Sort connections by recency, platform overlap, or name
    3. Create groups for easier management
    4. Adjust visibility settings for different connection groups

    Sharing Your Digital Identity

    Your SLID QR Code

    Every SLID account has a unique QR code for easy sharing:

    1. Go to your profile
    2. Click "Share My Profile"
    3. Download or share your QR code
    4. Others can scan this code to view your profile

    Your Unique Code

    You also have a unique alphanumeric code:

    1. Find your code on your profile page
    2. Share this code with others
    3. They can enter it in the "Find by Code" search feature

    Profile Privacy Options

    Control who sees what:

    1. Go to "Settings" > "Privacy"
    2. Choose who can view your:
    3. Connected accounts
    4. Content feed
    5. Connections list
    6. Profile information

    Privacy and Security

    Security Features

    SLID protects your digital identity with:

    • Two-factor authentication
    • Login alerts
    • Session management
    • Encryption of sensitive data
    • Regular security audits

    Enabling Two-Factor Authentication

    Set up 2FA for added security:

    1. Go to "Settings" > "Security"
    2. Click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication"
    3. Choose your preferred method (authenticator app or SMS)
    4. Follow the setup instructions
    5. Save your backup codes in a secure location

    Managing App Permissions

    Review and update platform permissions:

    1. Go to "Settings" > "Connected Accounts"
    2. Select a platform
    3. Review current permissions
    4. Click "Manage Permissions" to make changes
    5. Save your changes

    Troubleshooting

    Common Issues

    Can't Connect a Social Media Account - Check that you're using the correct login credentials - Ensure you're not in private browsing mode - Try disconnecting and reconnecting the account - Verify the platform's servers are online

    Content Not Displaying - Check your connection to the platform is still active - Refresh your SLID dashboard - Verify the content still exists on the original platform - Check your internet connection

    AI Assistant Not Responding - Refresh the page - Try rephrasing your question - Check if you've reached the daily query limit - Ensure your request is related to your social data

    Getting Help

    If you need assistance:

    1. Check the FAQ section in "Help"
    2. Use the AI assistant for general guidance
    3. Contact support through:
    4. In-app chat (business hours)
    5. Email at support@slid.example.com
    6. Knowledge base at help.slid.example.com

    Reporting Bugs

    If you encounter a bug:

    1. Go to "Settings" > "Help & Support"
    2. Click "Report a Bug"
    3. Describe what happened
    4. Include steps to reproduce the issue
    5. Add screenshots if possible

    Conclusion

    SLID empowers you to take control of your digital identity across multiple platforms. As you connect more accounts and explore the features, you'll discover new ways to streamline your social media management and enhance your online presence.

    We're constantly improving SLID based on user feedback. Feel free to share your experiences and suggestions through the feedback form in the app.

    Thank you for choosing SLID as your digital identity platform!
